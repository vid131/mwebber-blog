I'm Michael Webber. I run Foxboro Cable Access, the community media station in Foxborough, Massachusetts, and I live up the road in Cumberland, Rhode Island. This blog is my field notebook. It is not about one thing, because the work and the life are not one thing, but it does have a shape. Most of what I write falls into three piles:

**The Station.** Local media and the craft behind it. How a small station records a town's meetings and games, the tools and software (some of it built at my own desk), and where broadcasting and community media are heading. The Weekend Media Reads live here too.

**The Road.** Running, mostly along the Blackstone River, in all seasons and in various states of fitness. The road is where I think, and where my body keeps its honest ledger.

**Field Notes.** Everything noticed along the way. Places that are disappearing, people on the edge of the frame, small moments that asked for a second look. Ann & Hope before the signs came down. A shelter in the woods beside the running path. A stranger's flat tire in a dark parking lot.
